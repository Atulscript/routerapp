How to Log In to ACT Fibernet @ 192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1

Universal router access instructions

3 Quick Steps
1

Connect your device to the router

Make sure your PC, laptop, or smartphone is connected via an RJ-45 Ethernet cable or to the router's Wi-Fi network SSID.

💡
Pro Tip: Using an Ethernet cable provides a direct link and prevents signal dropouts during setup.
2

Open your web browser and navigate to 192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1

Open Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox and type http://192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1 into the URL bar, then press Enter.

3

Enter factory default username & password

When prompted by the login dialog, input the default credentials (usually admin / admin or admin / password).

ACT Fibernet @ 192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1 Support & Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for common connection issues

Diagnostics
🔄 Forgotten Admin Password?

Locate the physical RESET pinhole button on the router's rear panel. Press and hold with a paperclip for 10-15 seconds until all LED lights flash. This restores default factory credentials.

⚠️ 192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1 Refuses to Connect?

Disable mobile data on your phone and disconnect active VPN connections. Confirm your router's exact gateway IP using our Gateway Finder Tool.

Understanding Your ACT Fibernet ONT Indicator Lights

If your broadband shows "Connected, No Internet", glance at the front LED indicators on your fiber optical modem before logging in:

PON Light (Solid Green)

Optical connection with your ISP exchange is healthy and authenticated.

LOS Light (Blinking Red)

Loss of Signal: The optical fiber cord is loose, bent, or there is an ISP fiber cut.

LAN / WLAN (Blinking Green)

Data packets are actively transmitting between devices and the router.
