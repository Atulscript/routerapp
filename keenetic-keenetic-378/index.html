How to Log In & Set Up Your Keenetic Keenetic 378

  1. 1 Connect Your Device: Plug your PC into any LAN port on the Keenetic Keenetic 378 using an Ethernet cable, or connect to the default Wi-Fi SSID printed on the router label.
  2. 2 Navigate to Gateway IP: Launch your web browser and enter http://192.168.1.1 into the address bar.
  3. 3 Enter Factory Credentials: Enter username "admin" and password "admin", then click Login.
  4. 4 Configure Security & Password: Follow the on-screen Quick Setup wizard to customize your Wi-Fi SSID, set WPA3/WPA2-AES encryption, and establish a new master administrator password.

Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

Q. What is the default login for Keenetic Keenetic 378? ▼

The default IP address is http://192.168.1.1, default username is "admin", and default password is "admin".

Q. What ports and speeds does Keenetic Keenetic 378 support? ▼

The Keenetic Keenetic 378 is equipped with 1x 1G WAN, 4x 1G LAN Ports with an overall speed rating of Gigabit Class.

Q. How do I reset my Keenetic Keenetic 378 to factory settings? ▼

Press and hold the physical Reset button on the Keenetic Keenetic 378 for 10 to 15 seconds until the power LED blinks, resetting all settings to factory default.
