Why Can't I Connect to 10.0.0.1? (Top 4 Quick Fixes)

1. Check Your Physical Connection

Make sure your phone or laptop is actively connected to your router's Wi-Fi network or plugged directly into a LAN port with an Ethernet cable. If you are on cellular data (4G/5G), turn off mobile data first.

2. Disable Active VPNs & Proxies

Active VPN tunnels reroute your network traffic to a remote server, making local private IP addresses like 10.0.0.1 unreachable. Temporarily disconnect your VPN.

3. Avoid Typo Mistakes

Double check the URL bar. Do not type letters (like "l" instead of "1") and do not search for it in Google. Type http://10.0.0.1 directly in the browser address bar.

4. Verify Gateway Address

Your router might be on a different subnet (e.g. 192.168.0.1 or 10.0.0.1). Open Command Prompt on Windows and type ipconfig to confirm your Default Gateway.

How to Change Your Wi-Fi Password at 10.0.0.1

Once you have successfully logged in using your default credentials, securing your wireless network is simple:

  1. Navigate to the Wireless or Wi-Fi Settings menu tab on the left dashboard.
  2. Locate the SSID Name field to rename your network (e.g., Home_5G).
  3. Under Security Mode, select WPA2-PSK (AES) or WPA3-Personal.
  4. Type your new custom password in the Passphrase / Pre-Shared Key field.
  5. Click Save / Apply. Your connected devices will briefly disconnect and require the new password.
