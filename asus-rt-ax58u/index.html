How to Log In & Set Up Your ASUS RT-AX58U

  1. 1 Connect Your Device: Plug your PC into any LAN port on the ASUS RT-AX58U using an Ethernet cable, or connect to the default Wi-Fi SSID printed on the router label.
  2. 2 Navigate to Gateway IP: Launch your web browser and enter http://192.168.50.1 into the address bar.
  3. 3 Enter Factory Credentials: Enter username "admin" and password "admin", then click Login.
  4. 4 Configure Security & Password: Follow the on-screen Quick Setup wizard to customize your Wi-Fi SSID, set WPA3/WPA2-AES encryption, and establish a new master administrator password.

Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

Common troubleshooting solutions and login assistance

Q. What is the default login for ASUS RT-AX58U? ▼

The default IP address is http://192.168.50.1, default username is "admin", and default password is "admin".

Q. What ports and speeds does ASUS RT-AX58U support? ▼

The ASUS RT-AX58U is equipped with 1x 1G WAN, 4x 1G LAN, 1x USB 3.0 with an overall speed rating of AX3000 Dual-Band.

Q. How do I reset my ASUS RT-AX58U to factory settings? ▼

Press and hold the physical Reset button on the ASUS RT-AX58U for 10 to 15 seconds until the power LED blinks, resetting all settings to factory default.
