Xfinity Comcast @ 10.0.0.1 Support & Troubleshooting

Quick fixes for common connection issues

Diagnostics
🔄 Forgotten Admin Password?

Locate the physical RESET pinhole button on the router's rear panel. Press and hold with a paperclip for 10-15 seconds until all LED lights flash. This restores default factory credentials.

⚠️ 10.0.0.1 Refuses to Connect?

Disable mobile data on your phone and disconnect active VPN connections. Confirm your router's exact gateway IP using our Gateway Finder Tool.

Understanding Your Xfinity Comcast ONT Indicator Lights

If your broadband shows "Connected, No Internet", glance at the front LED indicators on your fiber optical modem before logging in:

PON Light (Solid Green)

Optical connection with your ISP exchange is healthy and authenticated.

LOS Light (Blinking Red)

Loss of Signal: The optical fiber cord is loose, bent, or there is an ISP fiber cut.

LAN / WLAN (Blinking Green)

Data packets are actively transmitting between devices and the router.

Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

Q. How do I log in to my Xfinity Comcast fiber router admin portal? ▼

Connect to your Xfinity Comcast Wi-Fi network, navigate to http://10.0.0.1 in your browser, and enter username "admin" and password "password".

Q. What should I do if the LOS light on my Xfinity Comcast router is blinking red? ▼

A blinking red LOS (Loss of Signal) light indicates an optical fiber cable disconnection or an ISP outage. Verify the thin yellow fiber cable is firmly seated, and contact Xfinity Comcast support if the issue persists.

Q. How can I change my Xfinity Comcast Wi-Fi password and network name? ▼

Log in to http://10.0.0.1, go to Network/WLAN → 2.4G/5G Settings, update the SSID and WPA Pre-Shared Key, then click Apply.
