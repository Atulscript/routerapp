    How to Log In & Set Up Your Ikanos Communications Ikanos Communications Enterprise Core Pro Gateway

    1. 1 Connect Your Device: Plug your PC into any LAN port on the Ikanos Communications Ikanos Communications Enterprise Core Pro Gateway using an Ethernet cable, or connect to the default Wi-Fi SSID printed on the router label.
    2. 2 Navigate to Gateway IP: Launch your web browser and enter http://192.168.1.1 into the address bar.
    3. 3 Enter Factory Credentials: Enter username "admin" and password "admin", then click Login.
    4. 4 Configure Security & Password: Follow the on-screen Quick Setup wizard to customize your Wi-Fi SSID, set WPA3/WPA2-AES encryption, and establish a new master administrator password.

    Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

    Common troubleshooting solutions and login assistance

    Q. What is the default login for Ikanos Communications Ikanos Communications Enterprise Core Pro Gateway? ▼

    The default IP address is http://192.168.1.1, default username is "admin", and default password is "admin".

    Q. What ports and speeds does Ikanos Communications Ikanos Communications Enterprise Core Pro Gateway support? ▼

    The Ikanos Communications Ikanos Communications Enterprise Core Pro Gateway is equipped with 1x GbE WAN, 4x GbE LAN with an overall speed rating of Gigabit Class.

    Q. How do I reset my Ikanos Communications Ikanos Communications Enterprise Core Pro Gateway to factory settings? ▼

    Press and hold the physical Reset button on the Ikanos Communications Ikanos Communications Enterprise Core Pro Gateway for 10 to 15 seconds until the power LED blinks, resetting all settings to factory default.
