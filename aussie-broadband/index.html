    Step-by-Step Aussie Broadband ONT Setup Guide

    1. 1 Connect your computer or smartphone to your Aussie Broadband router Wi-Fi or LAN port.
    2. 2 Open your browser and navigate to http://192.168.20.1 (or http://192.168.1.1 on select NetComm models).
    3. 3 Enter default Username: admin and Password: admin (or the unique password on the gateway card).
    4. 4 Configure NBN WAN connection type (IPoE/DHCP or PPPoE), Wi-Fi bands, and parental controls.

    Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

    Common troubleshooting solutions and login assistance

    Q. How do I log in to my Aussie Broadband fiber router admin portal? ▼

    Connect to your Aussie Broadband Wi-Fi network, navigate to http://192.168.20.1 in your browser, and enter username "admin" and password "admin".

    Q. What should I do if the LOS light on my Aussie Broadband router is blinking red? ▼

    A blinking red LOS (Loss of Signal) light indicates an optical fiber cable disconnection or an ISP outage. Verify the thin yellow fiber cable is firmly seated, and contact Aussie Broadband support if the issue persists.

    Q. How can I change my Aussie Broadband Wi-Fi password and network name? ▼

    Log in to http://192.168.20.1, go to Network/WLAN -> 2.4G/5G Settings, update the SSID and WPA Pre-Shared Key, then click Apply.
