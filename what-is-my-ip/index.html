Understanding Your Public IP Address

🌐 Public IPv4 vs Private Gateway IP Your Public IP is assigned by your Internet Service Provider and acts as your network's external postal address. Your private IP (192.168.1.1) stays inside your home network.
🔄 Why Does My Public IP Change? Most residential internet connections use Dynamic IP addressing or CGNAT. Your ISP reassigns IP addresses whenever your modem restarts or the DHCP lease refreshes.
📍 Can Someone Find My Exact Location from My IP? No. Public IP geolocation only identifies the ISP's nearest routing exchange or city hub — never your private residence or street address.
