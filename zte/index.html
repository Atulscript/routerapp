How to Log In to ZTE @ 192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1

Step-by-Step

Follow these three steps to access the web-based management dashboard of your ZTE router at http://192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1:

1

Connect your device to the router

Ensure your computer or phone is connected via Ethernet cable or to the router's Wi-Fi network.

💡 Pro Tip: Using an Ethernet cable ensures a stable setup without accidental disconnections during firmware updates.
2

Open your web browser and navigate to 192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1

Type http://192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1 in your browser's address bar and press Enter.

3

Enter factory default username and password

Enter the default credentials (usually admin / admin or admin / password) to open the control panel.

ZTE @ 192.168.1.1 / 192.168.0.1 Support & Troubleshooting

Help Guide
Forgotten Admin Password?

Press and hold the physical RESET button on the back of the router for 10 seconds to restore factory default settings.

192.168.1.1 Not Opening?

Make sure your device is connected to the right Wi-Fi SSID and not on cellular mobile data. Check our gateway IP list to confirm your address.
