    How to Log In & Configure Your Netgear Router

    1. 1 Connect your device to your Netgear router WiFi.
    2. 2 Open http://routerlogin.net or http://192.168.1.1 in a browser.
    3. 3 Enter username: admin and password: password.
    4. 4 Use the Genie or Nighthawk dashboard to manage your network settings.
    🔄 How to Hard Reset Netgear Router to Factory Defaults

    If you forgot your customized admin password or cannot reach the web portal, locate the physical RESET pinhole button on the back of your Netgear router. While powered on, press and hold the button with a paperclip for 10 to 15 seconds until the power LED begins flashing. Release the button and wait 2 minutes for the unit to reboot with factory default credentials (User: admin / Pass: password).

    Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

    Common troubleshooting solutions and login assistance

    Q. What is the default IP address for Netgear routers? ▼

    Most Netgear routers use 192.168.1.1 / routerlogin.net as their factory default gateway management address.

    Q. What is the factory default login password for Netgear? ▼

    The standard factory username is "admin" and default password is "password". Check your physical router label if these don't work.

    Q. How do I factory reset my Netgear router if I forgot the password? ▼

    Press and hold the physical RESET pinhole button on the back of your Netgear device with a paperclip for 10-15 seconds until all LED lights blink, restoring default credentials.
