🔄 Forgotten Admin Password?

Locate the physical RESET pinhole button on the router's rear panel. Press and hold with a paperclip for 10-15 seconds until all LED lights flash. This restores default factory credentials.

How to Change Your Wi-Fi Password at 192.168.86.1

Once you have successfully logged in using your default credentials, securing your wireless network is simple:

  1. Navigate to the Wireless or Wi-Fi Settings menu tab on the left dashboard.
  2. Locate the SSID Name field to rename your network (e.g., Home_5G).
  3. Under Security Mode, select WPA2-PSK (AES) or WPA3-Personal.
  4. Type your new custom password in the Passphrase / Pre-Shared Key field.
  5. Click Save / Apply. Your connected devices will briefly disconnect and require the new password.
