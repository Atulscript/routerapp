    How to Log In & Set Up Your MikroTik RB4011iGS+5HacQ2HnD-IN

    1. 1 Connect Your Device: Plug your PC into any LAN port on the MikroTik RB4011iGS+5HacQ2HnD-IN using an Ethernet cable, or connect to the default Wi-Fi SSID printed on the router label.
    2. 2 Navigate to Gateway IP: Launch your web browser and enter http://192.168.88.1 into the address bar.
    3. 3 Enter Factory Credentials: Enter username "admin" and password "Empty (no password)", then click Login.
    4. 4 Configure Security & Password: Follow the on-screen Quick Setup wizard to customize your Wi-Fi SSID, set WPA3/WPA2-AES encryption, and establish a new master administrator password.
