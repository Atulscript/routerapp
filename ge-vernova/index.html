    How to Log In & Configure Your GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) Router

    1. 1 Connect your computer to the GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) LAN port via Ethernet cable or wireless SSID.
    2. 2 Open your web browser and navigate to http://192.168.1.1 (or https://192.168.1.1).
    3. 3 Enter the default Username: admin and Password: admin.
    4. 4 Configure your network parameters, WAN connection, and administrative security settings.
    🔄 How to Hard Reset GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) Router to Factory Defaults

    If you forgot your customized admin password or cannot reach the web portal, locate the physical RESET pinhole button on the back of your GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) router. While powered on, press and hold the button with a paperclip for 10 to 15 seconds until the power LED begins flashing. Release the button and wait 2 minutes for the unit to reboot with factory default credentials (User: admin / Pass: admin).

    Frequently Asked Questions & Answers

    Common troubleshooting solutions and login assistance

    Q. What is the default IP address for GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) routers? ▼

    Most GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) routers use 192.168.1.1 as their factory default gateway management address.

    Q. What is the factory default login password for GE Vernova (MDS Orbit)? ▼

    The standard factory username is "admin" and default password is "admin". Check your physical router label if these don't work.

    Q. How do I factory reset my GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) router if I forgot the password? ▼

    Press and hold the physical RESET pinhole button on the back of your GE Vernova (MDS Orbit) device with a paperclip for 10-15 seconds until all LED lights blink, restoring default credentials.
